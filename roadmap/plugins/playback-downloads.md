# Playback, torrents and downloads

- ◻ **Default Torrent Filters** — automatically apply preferred subtitle/dub/codec filters to torrent searches.
- ◻ **Anti-Seeding** — prevent or limit torrent seeding.
- ◻ **Delete Watched Episodes** — remove already-watched local episodes to free disk space.
- ◻ **Debrid Toggle** — switch between configured debrid providers/API keys.
- ◻ **Custom Episode Metadata** — customize metadata used in episode handling.
