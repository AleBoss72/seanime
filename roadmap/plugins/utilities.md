# Utility plugins

- ◻ **Anime News** — show anime news inside Seanime.
- ◻ **Anime Quiz** — anime quiz/minigame.
- ◻ **Notes** — attach personal notes to media.
- ◻ **MAL Button** — quick MyAnimeList link from anime/manga pages.
- ◻ **MyDubList** — show whether a dub exists in selected languages.
- ◻ **Light Novel Reader** — read light novels directly inside Seanime.
- ◻ **Cookie Clicker** — tray minigame.
- ◻ **Marketplace Plus** — enhance marketplace browsing/management.
- ◻ **SeaUtils** — collection of general Seanime utilities.
